Fragrance / A Speckle project

Candie

A little nostalgia. A sugar-sweet world.

Identity · Packaging · Art direction
Behind the idea

A world of
its own.

A fragrance world that borrows the joy of a sweet shop. Candy-shaped bottles, soft colour and playful packaging turn a familiar everyday object into something to collect, gift and keep.

Story in progress — the following brief, challenge and approach are draft copy, awaiting the studio’s final project notes.

The brief

Imagine a fragrance collection with the personality of a favourite sweet. Bring the bottles, individual packs and gift set into one expressive world, with a recognisable identity running through every touchpoint.

The challenge

Make the candy reference feel immediate while keeping the fragrance itself at the centre. Give each bottle its own character, then bring those different shapes and colours together as a cohesive collection.

The solution

A playful wordmark, glossy finishes and a palette of pink, yellow and mint connect the range. Star-shaped and candy-wrapper windows frame the bottles, letting the product become part of the packaging’s graphic language.

How the idea takes shape

From a spark
to a whole world.

Start with a feeling

Sweet-shop nostalgia sets the tone: soft gradients, rounded forms and small star details create a world that feels cheerful and tactile.

Let the shape speak

LollyPop, Jelly Bean and Gummies each have a distinctive silhouette. The packaging gives those forms room to lead, with a shared identity tying the collection together.

Build the whole world

Candy, dessert glassware and dreamlike settings extend the idea beyond the box. From the gift set to the imagery, every composition carries the same playful spirit.
